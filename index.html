04 / A practical process

Keep it clear.
Keep it moving.

Start with the problem, agree on the scope, and build something that works in the real world.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Find the useful part.

Talk through your audience, your current tools, and what you want to make easier. Define the result before deciding on the features.

02 / DESIGN & BUILD

Make the idea tangible.

Work from an agreed direction into a real, interactive experience. Review progress and refine the details that matter.

03 / TEST & HAND OVER

Leave it in good hands.

Check the important flows, document how it works, and agree on any ongoing support before launch.
